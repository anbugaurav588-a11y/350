Company Profile

Founded in Brighton & Hove, England, FEX World is a specialized financial information consulting enterprise focusing on global financial market research and customized consulting services. We combine local UK financial regulatory knowledge with international market data resources to provide reliable financial reference information.

Our corporate mission is to eliminate information asymmetry in financial investment, help clients grasp market trends reasonably, avoid unnecessary investment risks and realize steady asset appreciation.

After years of development, we have built complete data collection channels covering stock, foreign exchange, fund and commodity markets, supporting long-term stable consulting output for both individual and institutional clients.
